🤖 AI-Powered Development: Supercharge Your Productivity with Cursor !

Cursor AI has rapidly become the go-to coding companion, offering a seamless and deeply integrated development workflow. This isn't just another tool; it's a revolutionary IDE that does more than help you start projects with best practices—it has the answers to almost anything you can throw at it.

Powered by various models, I'll showcase concrete examples of smart code generation and share the best practices that make Cursor truly shine, including Cursor Rules and the MCP server.

Have you ever wanted to generate apps without writing a single line of code but didn't know where to start? Or maybe you've tried and weren't convinced?

Join this talk and get ready to learn how to truly "vibe-code"! You'll leave inspired and ready to adopt the power of AI in your daily development.
